The CMVM EAT tokens (F0X-80), built and signed in VMPL0.

The layout is the tag:fr0ntierx.com,2026:cmvm-eat-v1 profile that cmvm-dice in uPolaris defines and cmvm-verify appraises: a monitor token signed by the monitor alias key, carrying the nonce, the monitor measurement, each partition’s nested token under submods, and the monitor’s endorsement of each partition’s measurement and alias key. Claim keys are the RFC 9711 registrations plus the profile’s private keys.

Signatures are COSE_Sign1 (RFC 9052) with ES384, untagged.
