RetireError 🔒
Why retire_guest_vcpu stopped. Every error before PageRevoke leaves the RMP and the VMSA registry as they were. NoMapping, Busy, VmsaAdjust, and InvalidTarget found on the re-check under the lock come after AP_DESTROY, so the host has halted the target plane and the guest must treat that CPU as gone and retry. PageRevoke and VmsaRelease come after the vCPU is retired.
