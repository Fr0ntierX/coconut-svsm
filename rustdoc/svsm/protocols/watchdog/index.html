Constants§

WATCHDOG_DEFAULT_RETRIES 🔒
Plain retries after a failed reclaim, before the forced one, when the slot was armed with the default policy.
WATCHDOG_INUSE_DEADLINE 🔒
How long one watchdog retire keeps retrying an RMPADJUST that returns FAIL_INUSE, in TSC ticks (about 0.3 ms on the lab part). Shorter than the helper’s default because a Busy retire is retried on the next tick anyway, and the retire holds up every later retire in its tick.
WATCHDOG_MAX_PAGES 🔒
Partition pages one slot can carry for the revoke on reclaim.
WATCHDOG_SLOTS 🔒
Slots in the watch table. The tick reports slots as bitmasks, and the parttest tick call packs two of them into one register, so this must stay at or below 16.

watchdog_arm 🔒
Watch the vCPU running the guest-created VMSA at vmsa_pa. Its heartbeat is the u64 at heartbeat_pa, and it is retired once that value has not changed for deadline TSC ticks. pages are revoked from every guest VMPL when it is retired. policy says how a failed retire is retried. Returns the slot index.
watchdog_disarm 🔒
Stop watching a slot and free it. An armed slot is simply dropped, and a finished one (reclaimed or failed) is cleared. A retrying slot is dropped too, which gives up the reclaim and leaves the vCPU as the last attempt left it. A slot whose retire is running is refused.
watchdog_status 🔒
watchdog_tick 🔒
One watchdog period, called from the watchdog vCPU. Checks every armed slot, retires the vCPUs past their deadline, and retries the reclaims that failed on an earlier tick. Retires run outside the table lock, so arm, disarm and status calls on other vCPUs never wait on the host’s part of a retire.
