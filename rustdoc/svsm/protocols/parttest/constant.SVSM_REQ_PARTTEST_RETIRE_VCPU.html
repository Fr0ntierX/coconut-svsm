Constant SVSM_REQ_PARTTEST_RETIRE_VCPU

Source 
const SVSM_REQ_PARTTEST_RETIRE_VCPU: u32 = 7;
Expand description

Retire a guest vCPU and revoke a partition’s pages (F0X-75 watchdog reclaim), through core::retire_guest_vcpu. The guest must take the target CPU offline first.

In: rcx = the target’s VMSA GPA. rdx = GPA of a 4K page list, or 0 for none: a u64 count (at most RETIRE_MAX_PAGES) followed by that many 4K GPAs. r8 bits 0-62 = FAIL_INUSE deadline in TSC ticks (0 for the default), bit 63 = retire even if the host refuses AP_DESTROY.

Out, on success and on every error: rcx = AP_DESTROY ticks, rdx = ticks from the first RMPADJUST on the VMSA to success, r8 = pages revoked (bits 0-15) | FAIL_INUSE retries (16-47) | AP_DESTROY refused (63).

Errors: INVALID_PARAMETER (not a guest-created in-use VMSA, the caller’s own vCPU, or a bad list), INVALID_ADDRESS (a list entry is not a guest page or is a VMSA or calling area, entry index in rdx), BUSY (FAIL_INUSE past the deadline), and the protocol codes below, whose detail goes in rdx. See RetireError for what each leaves behind.
